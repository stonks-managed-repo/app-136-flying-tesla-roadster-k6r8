About the token

A roadster that left the road for good

Flying Tesla Roadster ($FTR) is a token on the TON blockchain, launched on Stonks.

It takes the most recognisable car of the electric era, lifts it clear off the asphalt and plants it on TON — the fast, low-fee network that lives inside Telegram. The result is a community token with a simple, shareable idea: the roadster is flying, and it's doing it on-chain.

$FTR launched on Stonks, the TON launchpad where tokens go live and trade directly from a wallet. No forms, no waiting rooms — just the token, the chart and the people who showed up for it.

How to buy

Pre-flight checklist 3 steps

Getting $FTR takes a TON wallet, a little TON (or GRAM) and one visit to Stonks.

  1. 1

    Get a TON wallet

    Set up a wallet that supports TON — for example Tonkeeper, or Wallet inside Telegram. Back up your recovery phrase and never share it with anyone.

  2. 2

    Fund it with TON or GRAM

    Buy TON on an exchange and withdraw it to your wallet address, or top up directly in-wallet. You'll use TON (or GRAM) to swap into $FTR and to cover the small network fees.

  3. 3

    Buy on Stonks

    Open app.stonks.cash, connect your wallet, find Flying Tesla Roadster ($FTR), enter the amount and confirm the transaction. $FTR lands in your wallet.
